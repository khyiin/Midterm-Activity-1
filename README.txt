HAVENA — FIVE-PAGE STANDALONE WEBSITE

Extract this entire ZIP, then open index.html in your browser.
Keep all folders together. No installation, framework, or internet is needed.

Pages: index.html (Home), shop.html, about.html, contact.html, cart.html.
CSS: css/shared.css and separate page files under css/pages/.
JavaScript: js/catalog.js (products) and js/main.js (interactions).
Images and fonts: assets/images/ and assets/fonts/.

The bag stays within the current browser tab while moving between pages.
When opened directly from disk, tab memory carries the bag between files.
On a static web server, sessionStorage keeps the bag for that tab.
Search, category filtering, product details, quantities, and removal work.
Online checkout is not connected. Contact and newsletter forms validate
input but do not send messages or create subscriptions.
About and Contact supporting text was drafted for this download; replace
it with approved business copy before use. No contact details were invented.
Photography is the matching replacement imagery from the Havena preview.
Fonts include their embedded notices; licensing is noted in FONT-LICENSE.txt.
